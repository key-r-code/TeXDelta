# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TeXDelta welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and precise. Assume good intent, accept correction gracefully, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work. Do not harass, insult, threaten, expose private information, or use sexualized or discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's private repository reporting features. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
